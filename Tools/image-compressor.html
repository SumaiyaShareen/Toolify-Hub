Free Online Image Compressor

ToolifyHub Image Compressor lets you reduce the file size of JPG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser. You can choose the compression quality and download the optimized image immediately.

How to Compress an Image

  1. Upload your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Adjust the image quality slider.
  3. Select your preferred output format.
  4. Click the "Compress Image" button.
  5. Compare the original and compressed file sizes.
  6. Download your compressed image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the image compressor free? +
Yes. ToolifyHub's image compressor is free to use.
Are my images uploaded? +
No. The compression process happens directly inside your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API.
Which formats are supported? +
JPG, PNG and WebP images are supported.
Can I choose the compression quality? +
Yes. Use the quality slider to choose between smaller file size and higher visual quality.
