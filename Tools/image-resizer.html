Free Online Image Resizer

ToolifyHub's image resizer lets you quickly change the dimensions of an image without installing additional software. Upload an image, choose your desired dimensions and download the resized file.

How to resize an image

  1. Upload your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Enter the desired width and height.
  3. Keep aspect ratio enabled to prevent distortion.
  4. Click "Resize Image".
  5. Download your resized image.

Supported formats

This tool supports common image formats including JPG, PNG and WebP.

Image Resizer FAQ

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. You can resize images online without installing software.

Are my images uploaded?

Image processing is performed in your browser. The page does not need to upload the image to a server.

Can I maintain the aspect ratio?

Yes. Keep "Maintain aspect ratio" enabled while changing dimensions.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP are supported.
