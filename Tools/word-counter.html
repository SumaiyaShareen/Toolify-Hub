Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Word Counter free?

Yes. You can use the ToolifyHub Word Counter without creating an account.

Can I paste a long article?

Yes. The tool is designed to analyze normal articles, essays, assignments and other text directly in your browser.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No ToolifyHub server upload is required for the counting functions. The analysis runs directly in your browser.
