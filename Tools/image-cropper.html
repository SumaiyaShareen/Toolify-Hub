Free Online Image Cropper

ToolifyHub's Image Cropper lets you crop JPG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser. You can select a custom area or use common aspect ratios such as 1:1, 16:9 and 4:3.

How to Crop an Image

1. Upload: Select or drag your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
2. Select: Drag on the image to select the area you want to crop.
3. Download: Choose the output format and download your cropped image.
