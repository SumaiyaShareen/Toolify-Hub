Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolifyHub handles information when you use our website and online tools.

Last Updated: September 22, 2026
This Privacy Policy may be updated when ToolifyHub introduces new features, services or third-party integrations.

1. Information We Collect

ToolifyHub is designed to provide online utilities with a simple user experience. Depending on how you use the website, different types of information may be processed.

Information You Provide

If you contact us, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address and message.

Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, ToolifyHub may use standard technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location and pages visited when analytics or other website services are enabled.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate website and service purposes, including:

  • Providing and maintaining ToolifyHub.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Understanding how visitors use the website.
  • Detecting abuse, fraud or technical problems.
  • Improving existing tools and developing new features.

3. File Processing

Several ToolifyHub tools are designed to process files directly in your web browser. When a tool operates entirely in the browser, the selected file does not need to be uploaded to a ToolifyHub server for the conversion or processing itself.

Important

Browser-based processing does not mean that every feature on the website is necessarily local. Third-party services, analytics or future server-based features may have different data practices. Check the relevant tool or service information before processing sensitive files.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ToolifyHub may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support website functionality and improve the user experience.

Third-party services, including analytics or advertising platforms, may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. Advertising

ToolifyHub may display advertisements in the future to support the operation and development of the website.

If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is used, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure advertisements according to its own policies.

Advertising Disclosure

Advertising practices may change as ToolifyHub develops. This Privacy Policy will be updated when relevant advertising services are introduced.

6. Third-Party Services

ToolifyHub may use third-party services for functions such as hosting, analytics, advertising, security, forms, fonts or software libraries.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies. ToolifyHub does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

7. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through the website.

However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting passwords, financial information or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact forms or tools unless the service specifically supports it.

8. Children's Privacy

ToolifyHub is a general-purpose website and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us unnecessarily, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

9. External Links

ToolifyHub may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites operated by third parties.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of an external website before providing it with personal information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to ToolifyHub, applicable requirements or third-party services.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how ToolifyHub handles information, please contact us through our Contact page.

Have a Privacy Question?

Contact ToolifyHub if you have a question about this policy or our website.
